## Reminders

- One receipt for each payment, even a small one.
- A reimbursement needs a receipt however it is paid, including Zelle or check.
- Give the client their copy right away. Keep yours with the client's records (section 10 of the agreement).
- Write the payment in the "payments received" table on that week's invoice.
