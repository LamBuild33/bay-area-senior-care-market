# Visit Note Form - [Last Name] Home Support

October 5, 2026

A short note to write after each visit and email to the client. Copy the form below into Notes on your phone, fill it in, and send it. Section 10 of the agreement says what the note covers: what we did, what the client managed on their own, and anything I noticed.

## The form

```text
Visit note
Date:
Start time - end time:
What we did:
What you managed on your own:
Anything I noticed:
Drives or purchases today (miles, items and amount, receipt kept):
Next visit:
```

## Example (not a real client)

```text
Visit note
Date: Tuesday, October 13
Start time - end time: 10:00 am - 2:15 pm
What we did: Made lunch together. Went to the pharmacy and the grocery store. Sorted this week's mail and added two appointments to the calendar.
What you managed on your own: Walked to the mailbox. Wrote the grocery list yourself.
Anything I noticed: The front porch step felt loose. You said your knee was sore this morning.
Drives or purchases today (miles, items and amount, receipt kept): 6.2 miles to the pharmacy and store. Groceries $38.40 on your card, receipt kept.
Next visit: Friday, October 16, 10:00 am.
```

## Reminders

- Write it the same day, while you remember.
- Keep it factual: what you did and saw. No diagnoses and no medical advice.
- Send it only to the client and the people they initialed on the intake sheet.
- Your start and end times are what you bill from, so write them every time.
- If something looks wrong, tell the client and their family or call for help (section 12 of the agreement). Do not wait for the note.
