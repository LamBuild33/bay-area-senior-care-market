## How to fill this in

**How the client pays.** By check, cash or Zelle, within 7 days of the date sent.

**Worked examples** (made-up numbers, at $35 an hour):

1. **Time to the nearest minute.** You arrive at 10:00 and leave at 2:20. That is 4 hours 20 minutes, or 4 + 20 ÷ 60 = 4.333 hours. 4.333 × $35 = $151.67.
2. **Shortest visit applies.** The shortest visit is 4 hours, but you stayed 3 hours 10 minutes. Bill 4 hours: 4 × $35 = $140.00.
3. **Mileage.** 6.2 miles at 76 cents a mile: 6.2 × $0.76 = $4.71.
4. **Purchases.** Groceries of $38.40 paid on the client's card are listed but not added. A $12.00 item paid on your own card is added to the total.

**Keep a copy** of every invoice, and every receipt, with the client's records (section 10 of the agreement).
